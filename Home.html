🌸⛩️ Anime Inventor 🌸⛩️

While the invention of anime cannot be attributed to a single individual, Osamu Tezuka is widely celebrated as the "Godfather of Anime" and the "God of Manga" for establishing the visual foundations of this art form. Its earliest roots trace back to the short clip "Katsudo Shashin" in 1907 and the first professional productions in 1917, though the industry's true turning point came in 1963 when Tezuka produced the first modern television series, "Astro Boy." Since that breakthrough, legendary creators have propelled anime and manga to global stardom. This includes Hayao Miyazaki, the co-founder of Studio Ghibli and creator of timeless cinematic masterpieces, and Akira Toriyama, the visionary behind "Dragon Ball," which served as the primary inspiration for the "Shonen" genre. Other titans of the industry include Eiichiro Oda, creator of "One Piece"—the best-selling manga in history—and Masashi Kishimoto, whose series "Naruto" played a pivotal role in popularizing anime culture worldwide. More recently, creators like Hajime Isayama have pushed the medium further with mature, complex epics such as "Attack on Titan."
